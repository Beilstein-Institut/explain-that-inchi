<objective>
Implement Feature 1 — Click-to-Pin (Freeze) Highlights. Clicking an InChI chunk (layer or sub-token) freezes its highlight + explanation so the user can inspect the molecule canvas; any subsequent click anywhere, or Esc, releases. The store hover gate is the single enforcement point.

Purpose: Today highlights are purely transient (vanish on mouseleave); pinning lets users hold a highlight while they look at the drawing.
Output: `pinned` store state machine, click wiring in InchiSection/LayerText, pinned precedence in the highlight hook and explanation card, the locked-state ring + release hint, and real-fixture tests.
</objective>
