InChIKey anatomy (pinned — verified against InChI Trust spec):
  Position  0-13  : skeleton hash (14 chars)
  Position  14    : hyphen (NOT a segment)
  Position  15-22 : remaining-layers hash (8 chars)
  Position  23    : flag — 'S' (standard) or 'N' (non-standard)
  Position  24    : version — 'A'
  Position  25    : hyphen (NOT a segment)
  Position  26    : protonation char (e.g. 'N' for neutral)

Benzene fixture: 'UHOVQNZJYSORNB-UHFFFAOYSA-N'
  key.slice(0,14)  === 'UHOVQNZJYSORNB'  (skeleton)
  key[14]          === '-'               (hyphen, not segment)
  key.slice(15,23) === 'UHFFFAOY'        (hash)
  key.slice(23,24) === 'S'              (flag)
  key.slice(24,25) === 'A'              (version)
  key[25]          === '-'              (hyphen, not segment)
  key.slice(26,27) === 'N'             (protonation)
  Total length     === 27
